## Answer (verbatim Gemma output)

Traditional valuation methods are not suitable for VC-funded companies because they deal with different types of companies [S1]. Traditional methods consider aspects like markets, ongoing operations, cash flows, and capital structure, whereas VC-funded companies involve emerging markets, changing business models, negative cash flows, and specific capital structures such as no borrowing capacity, equity, preferred stock (liquidation preferences), common stock (stock options), and future rounds [S1].
