> Step 5 — Exit Returns as Percentage of Fund
> The fund size is a large driver to what is required for an investment to return the
> fund.
> 
> The above example needed $700mm exit to return a $40mm fund.
> 
> This implies
> a $350mm exit to return a $20mm fund, a 50% haircut.
> 
> The same investment of
> $750k would require a $4.3bn exit for a $250mm fund, aka VERY UNLIKELY.
> 
> Hence,
> why a $250mm Seed fund is likely going to have a check size range of $2mm+.
> 
> A
> fund is generally not going to invest less than 1% of the fund in any company.
> 
> Most Seed funds are targeting a 3x – 5x gross return, which implies $120mm-$200mm
> for Harlem Capital’s $40mm fund.
> 
> If we assume for simplicity, HCP on average has
> 5% ownership at exit, this implies $2.4bn-$4.0bn of total exit value.
> 
> Now it begins to
> be clear why Unicorns are such a focus for VC.
> 
> A fund may not need unicorns to
> return the fund, but it will need billions in value creation to meet the 3x-5x return
> goals.
