# Ownership Targets

> Seed valuations are set by the ownership a fund needs, not revenue.
> Topic: [[index#Valuation|Valuation]] · From: [[Harlem Capital Return the Fund Post]]

## Summary
Ownership targets determine the valuation for seed-stage investments rather than relying on revenue multiples. Investors expect to take a specific percentage of ownership in exchange for their investment, and this expectation varies based on the size of the fund.

## Key points
* Seed valuations are set by the ownership targets a fund needs, not by revenue [^1].
* Founders should generally expect to give up 20% to 30% ownership in the Seed round [^1].
* Larger funds ($100mm+) typically have 10% to 20% ownership targets, while microfunds ($25mm – $100mm) target 5% to 10% [^1].
* If a fund invests $750k, it implies only investing in companies with a post-money valuation range of $7.5mm to $15mm to meet ownership targets [^1].
* The assumed Seed round dilution is often 25% (the midpoint of 20% – 30%), with subsequent rounds stepping down 5% each round [^2].
* Investors may reserve 30% to 40% of their fund for follow-ons to secure pro-rata rights in winners [^2].
* A fund may need billions in value creation to meet 3x-5x return goals [^3].
