# Management Fees and Carried Interest

> The ~2% management fee and 20% carried interest.
> Topic: [[index#Fund Structure|Fund Structure]] · From: [[Break Into VC Book]]

## Summary
Management fees and carried interest are key compensation structures in venture capital funds. Management fees are charged to limited partners to cover salaries, while carried interest represents the share of profits partners receive after initial investments are returned.

## Key points
*   Partners charge a management fee of about 2 percent to limited partners to cover salaries and staff [^1].
*   Carried interest is commonly 20 percent of future profits in venture capital [^2].
*   Carried Interest is defined as the share of profits partners receive after the amount invested has been returned [^2].
*   The 20 percent portion of profits received by partners is referred to as carried interest [^1].
*   Investors receive 80 percent of profits when limited partners receive 100 percent of their money back [^1].
*   The larger the firm’s stake in the company, the larger CynMerc’s returns are when the company exits [^1].
