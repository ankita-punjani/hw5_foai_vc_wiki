## Overview
A beginner's guide to venture capital careers that also teaches the fundamentals. It covers what VC is and how funds work, growth equity, venture debt, startup metrics, accounting, returns and valuation (including the VC method and pre-revenue methods), and interview preparation, plus interviews with analysts who broke in. Its glossary is the main source for [[VC Glossary]].
