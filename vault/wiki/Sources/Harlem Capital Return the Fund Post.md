## Overview
A seed fund's public walk-through of venture math (Harlem Capital, Medium, 2020), written for founders. It goes step by step through check size, ownership targets, dilution, exit value, and whether one investment can "return the fund". This wiki uses it as a worked example with real numbers from one $40mm fund, not as a profile of the firm.
