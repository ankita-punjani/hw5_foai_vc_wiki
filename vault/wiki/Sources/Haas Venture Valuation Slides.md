## Overview
Lecture slides from Berkeley Haas VCPE 295B (Class 5, Sep 23, 2026) on venture valuation. They cover value vs price vs valuation, why startups can't be valued like mature companies, comparables, the Venture Capital Method, projection reflection and scenario analysis, worked through the Mekasutra Games case. They also include the 10-company portfolio example behind the power law.
