# SaaS Metrics

> MRR, ARR, burn, CLV, CAC, and revenue retention.
> Topic: [[index#Startup Metrics|Startup Metrics]] · From: [[Break Into VC Book]]

## Summary
SaaS metrics are crucial measurements used to evaluate the performance and health of a subscription-based business. These metrics include revenue figures like MRR and ARR, retention measures like churn, and financial ratios such as CLV to CAC. Understanding these metrics helps determine a company's ability to generate sustainable revenue and manage customer relationships effectively.

## Key points
*   Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) is the sum of all contract revenue a business can rely on for the next month [^1].
*   Annualized Run Rate (ARR) is calculated by multiplying the Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) by 12 [^1].
*   The burn rate indicates how much cash a business typically spends in a given month or year [^1].
*   The Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) represents the projected revenue a customer will bring to the company during their lifetime [^2].
*   The CLV to CAC ratio shows how many dollars are brought in for every dollar spent on sales and marketing to acquire a customer [^3].
*   Customer acquisition cost (CAC) = sales and marketing spend ÷ new customers; e.g. $300,000 ÷ 300 customers = $1,000 CAC, and lower is better [^6].
*   Dollar Revenue Retention (DRR) measures the amount of revenue retained from customers in a given period [^4].

## Worked example
1. When a company has 40 annual subscriptions valued at $12,000 per contract, the monthly recurring revenue is $40,000 [^5].
1. The Annualized Run Rate (ARR) is found by multiplying the MRR by 12, resulting in $480,000 [^5].
