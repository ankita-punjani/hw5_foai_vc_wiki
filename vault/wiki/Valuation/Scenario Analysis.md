# Scenario Analysis

> Probability-weighted exit scenarios with dilution and time.
> Topic: [[index#Valuation|Valuation]] · From: [[Haas Venture Valuation Slides]]

## Summary
Scenario analysis values a deal by listing a few possible exits (big win, modest sale, small sale, zero), giving each a probability, and applying the dilution and time each path involves. The probability-weighted result shows both a value and "the shape of the risk".

## Key points
* Goals: estimate a valuation, understand the impact of key assumptions, consider the shape of the risk, and give the partnership a shared language [^1].
* Steps: pick end-state scenarios; consider dilution from later rounds; determine probabilities and time; analyse valuation and risk profile. Anchor assumptions with data (e.g. PitchBook, comparables) [^1].
* Mekasutra base case: invest $5.0m at a $9.5m pre-money → 34% ownership, diluted to 26% and 21% if more rounds are needed [^2].
* Exits $600M / $100M / $20M / $0 with probabilities 2% / 10% / 30% / 58% give ownership values $124m / $26m / $7m / $0, i.e. 25x / 5x / 1.4x / 0 [^2].
* Probable value is $7.2m on $5m invested, only 1.4x: a 42% chance of 1.4x+ and a 2% "lottery ticket" at 25x [^2].
* What-ifs change the answer: with no further cash needed, ~2.0x; at a $4.5m pre-money raising $0.5m, 4.2x (the lottery ticket becomes 72x) [^3].
* More than a price: it is "what you must believe", the key financial risks, and a language for risk inside and outside the partnership [^4].

## Worked example
1. Probable values: $124m × 2% ≈ $2.5m; $26m × 10% = $2.6m; $7m × 30% = $2.1m; $0 × 58% = $0 [^2].
1. Sum ≈ $7.2m ÷ $5m invested ≈ 1.4x expected multiple [^2].
1. Compare the three methods on Mekasutra: VC method $9.6m, Projection Reflection $7m, scenario analysis $4.5m+ pre-money [^5].
